Open Salamander Samandarin

Open Salamander Samandarin is an unofficial, experimental open-source fork of
Open Salamander, created by Ondrej Kotas (KRtekTM) with AI-assisted / vibe-coding development.
It is intended for adventurous users; please expect rough edges and verify the
release notes before relying on it for important work.
