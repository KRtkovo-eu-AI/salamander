and only ordinary Files-menu use. A simple diagnostic pending close passed cleanly;
other production pending closes warned. This is not claimed as universally clean
Debug shutdown or a proven allocator-origin fix. Logs preserve both comparisons;
heap.cpp and the existing512-byte suppression remain byte-unchanged. Earlier S22
ZIP shutdown warning is also retained as documented; it was not retested here.
