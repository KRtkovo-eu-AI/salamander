PictView Viewer: WIC-decoded image formats

PictView queries the local Windows Imaging Component (WIC) decoder inventory at start-up. The detected extensions are used for both viewing and thumbnails, so installed WIC codecs are supported without a hard-coded extension list.

ExtensionFormat description
All extensions advertised by installed WIC decodersSamandarin addition: PictView enumerates IWICBitmapDecoderInfo::GetFileExtensions at start-up and updates viewer and thumbnail registrations from that system inventory.
BMP, DIB, RLEWindows Bitmap, through the built-in WIC BMP decoder.
GIFGraphics Interchange Format, including animation frames exposed by WIC.
ICOWindows Icon, through the built-in WIC icon decoder.
JPG, JPE, JPEG, JFIFJPEG / JFIF, through the built-in WIC JPEG decoder.
PNGPortable Network Graphics, through the built-in WIC PNG decoder.
TIF, TIFFTagged Image File Format, through the built-in WIC TIFF decoder.
JXR, WDPJPEG XR / HD Photo (Windows Media Photo), through the built-in WIC codec.
DDSDirectDraw Surface variants accepted by the WIC DDS decoder.
WebP, HEIF/HEIC, DNG/RAW and other optional image formatsAvailable when their WIC codec is installed on the local Windows system; their extensions are discovered automatically and are not treated as universal support.
